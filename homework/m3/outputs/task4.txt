    ## Basics
    - Topics are split into partitions; ordering is guaranteed only within a partition.
    - The record key decides the partition, so all events for one key stay in order.
    - Retention is by time or size, not by consumption, so consumers can re-read data.
    - Replication factor 3 + `min.insync.replicas=2` + `acks=all` survives one broker loss without data loss.
    
    ## Consumer groups
    - One consumer per partition within a group; extra consumers stay idle.
    - Offsets are committed per group and partition in `__consumer_offsets`.
    - Rebalances happen when a consumer joins, leaves or exceeds `max.poll.interval.ms`; cooperative sticky assignment avoids stop-the-world rebalances.
    - Lag = log end offset - committed offset; alert when it keeps growing.
    
    ## Exactly-once
    - The idempotent producer (`enable.idempotence=true`) removes duplicates from retries.
    - Transactions span partitions and commit consumer offsets atomically.
    - Consumers need `isolation.level=read_committed` to skip aborted records.
    - Exactly-once ends at Kafka: external side effects still need idempotent handlers.
